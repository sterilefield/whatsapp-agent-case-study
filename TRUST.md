# Trust, scope and limitations

> [!WARNING]
> Sanitised public showcase. Not for clinical use as-is, not legal or regulatory advice, and not a product. Last verified 21 Oct 2026.

## What this repository is
Anonymised case study (documentation). Hiring managers, founders and engineers evaluating how a governed WhatsApp and Telegram agent platform is designed and operated.

## Out of scope
It contains no production code, prompts, configuration or customer data, and it does not claim the platform drove sales. `demo/` and `site/` are small teaching models written for publication, on invented data.

## Limitations
- One client, one market (Hyderabad real estate); results are not a benchmark.
- The client is anonymised; details that could identify the client or its people are left out on purpose.
- Figures are as of the dates in the timeline and are shared with the client's permission.

## Data provenance
No customer data, phone numbers, names, code, prompts or configuration. Aggregate figures only, shared with the client's written permission. Every release passes five gates before it is public: G1 allow-list of files, G2 secret and deny-list scan, G3 fresh history (no private commits travel), G4 fresh-context AI review (an agent that did not write the material), G5 owner approval (the human check) with a licence check. See the [publication policy](https://github.com/sterilefield/.github/blob/main/PUBLICATION_POLICY.md).

## Privacy and regulation
No personal data is processed by anything in this repository. Where the text discusses India's DPDP Act and Rules, systems are described as *designed toward* those requirements, never as certified or compliant.
